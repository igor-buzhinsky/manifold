# RPG Audio

Sounds from [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) by Kenney (Kenney Vleugels, kenney.nl).

- **License**: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), as stated in the pack's `License.txt`, which is kept here.
  Credit is not required.
- **Contents**: `doorOpen_1.ogg` and `doorOpen_2.ogg` of the pack's `Audio` folder, used as sounds of doors
  that start to open. The other sounds of the pack are not included.
- **Processing**: none. The files are as they come in the pack (OGG Vorbis).
