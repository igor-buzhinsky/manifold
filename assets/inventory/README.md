# Inventory

Icons of items, 64 x 64 PNG files with a transparent background, listed under `ITEM_ICONS` of
`assets/asset_mapping.json` by the keys of their items, see `assets/items.json`. They are grouped by the kind of
item:

- `gems/`: gems, each as a large and a small gem. A small gem is worth a quarter of a large one, so it is drawn with a
  quarter of its volume, about 1.59 times narrower.

Inside each group, the files of each pack are in a directory of their own, with a `README.md` that tells where they
come from, their license, which files were taken and how they were processed.
